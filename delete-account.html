Account & Data Deletion

Special Sathi respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Special Sathi account directly from the mobile application.

Steps to delete your account:

  1. Open the Special Sathi app.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the information displayed on the confirmation screen.
  6. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.
Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once the deletion process has been completed, your account may not be recoverable.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When you permanently delete your account, account-associated personal data will be deleted, subject to applicable legal and security retention requirements.

  • Mobile number and account information
  • Name and basic profile information
  • Date of birth, gender, education and occupation information
  • Matrimony and profile details
  • Profile photographs
  • Location information associated with your account
  • Uploaded identity and disability verification documents
  • Account-related preferences and settings
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where necessary for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Legal or regulatory compliance
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Resolving disputes
  • Enforcing our terms and policies
  • Maintaining necessary technical or security records

Any retained information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

Account Deactivation

If you do not want to permanently delete your account, Special Sathi also provides an Account Deactivation option.

Deactivation is different from permanent account deletion. A deactivated account may be reactivated according to the application's available reactivation process.

Unable to Access the App?

If you cannot access your Special Sathi account and need to request account deletion, please contact the Special Sathi support team through the contact or support information provided by Special Sathi.

You may be asked to provide sufficient information to verify account ownership before a deletion request can be processed.

Security Notice: Never send your password, OTP, or other authentication credentials when requesting account deletion.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Special Sathi collects, uses, stores and protects personal information, please refer to the Special Sathi Privacy Policy.

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent and may not be reversible after the deletion process has been completed.

By requesting account deletion, you acknowledge that your account and associated personal data may no longer be available after the deletion process is completed.
