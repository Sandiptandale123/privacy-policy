# Special Sathi – Account & Data Deletion **Last Updated: September 22, 2026** Special Sathi respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to permanently delete their account and associated personal data. ## How to Delete Your Account You can permanently delete your Special Sathi account directly from the app. ### Steps: 1. Open the **Special Sathi** app. 2. Go to your **Profile**. 3. Open **Settings**. 4. Select **Delete Account**. 5. Review the information shown on the confirmation screen. 6. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account. Once the deletion process is completed, your account will no longer be available for use. ## Data That Will Be Deleted When you permanently delete your account, the following account-associated data will be deleted, subject to the retention requirements described below: * Mobile number and account information * Name and basic profile information * Date of birth, gender, education and occupation information * Matrimony and profile details * Profile photographs * Location information associated with your account * Uploaded identity and disability verification documents * Account-related preferences and settings * Other personal information associated with your account ## Data That May Be Retained Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for: * Legal or regulatory compliance * Fraud prevention and security * Resolving disputes * Enforcing our terms and policies * Maintaining necessary technical or security records Any retained information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required. ## Account Deactivation If you do not want to permanently delete your account, Special Sathi also provides an **Account Deactivation** option where available. Deactivation is different from permanent account deletion. A deactivated account may be reactivated according to the application's available reactivation process. ## Unable to Access the App? If you cannot access your Special Sathi account and need to request account deletion, please contact the Special Sathi support team through the contact/support information provided on the official Special Sathi website or app. Please include sufficient information to help us verify your account ownership. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or other sensitive authentication credentials. ## Important Account deletion is permanent and may not be reversible after the deletion process has been completed. For more information about how Special Sathi collects, uses and protects personal information, please refer to the **Special Sathi Privacy Policy**. © 2026 Special Sathi. All rights reserved.
